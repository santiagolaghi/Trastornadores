TNT uses self-hosted fonts to avoid an extra connection on every app screen.

- Archivo Black, by Omnibus-Type: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/archivoblack
- Inter, by Rasmus Andersson: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter

Both families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The font
binaries were downloaded from Google Fonts; their copyright notices are embedded
in the fonts. `inter.woff2` is the Latin variable font, including Spanish accents.
